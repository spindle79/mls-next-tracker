## Key Takeaways

1. **The ceiling is #6, the floor is #15.** That's a huge range, which shows how much the remaining 8 games matter.

2. **Top 6 requires near-perfection.** The Glens need to win 7 or 8 of their remaining 8 games to have a realistic shot at the top 6. That means pulling off at least 2-3 upsets against teams currently ranked above them (Burlingame, Modesto, Elk Grove, or Napa).

3. **The 4 "should win" games are critical.** Lamorinda (81%), Ballistic United (85%), SF Seals (85%), and Breakers FC (58%) are the games the Glens must take. Dropping any of these effectively eliminates top-6 hopes.

4. **Napa United at home (04/26) is the swing game.** At 36.9% win probability, this is the most winnable of the "tough" games. Winning this one could be the difference between finishing #10 and #12.

5. **The realistic expectation is #11-12.** The model projects 22 PTS and rank #12. To meaningfully improve on that, the Glens need to win at least one of the four games they're predicted to lose.

6. **The gap to 6th is deceptively large.** Elk Grove is only 2 points ahead now, but they have 11 games remaining and are unbeaten (3-0-3). They're projected to reach 39 PTS. The real targets for the Glens are ALBION SC Merced (projected 27), Diablo Valley (25), Atletico Santa Rosa (25), Napa United (25), and De Anza Force (23) -- the #7-#11 cluster.
