**The three games that matter most, in order:**

1. **Elk Grove (04/18 away)** -- The single highest-leverage game. A win or even a draw changes the math entirely.
2. **Napa United (04/26 home)** -- The most realistic rival to overtake. Must win at home.
3. **Modesto Ajax (04/04 away)** -- An upset here is the kind of result that makes a top-6 run possible.

**The four games you cannot afford to drop:**
- Lamorinda (03/22), Ballistic United (04/12), San Francisco Seals (04/19), Breakers FC (04/19) -- these are the floor. Winning all four is table stakes.

**The uncomfortable truth:** The model projects the Glens finishing #12 with 22 PTS. Top 6 requires at minimum 30+ PTS, which means going roughly 7-1 over the remaining 8 games. That is a massive step up from the current 3-5-1 record. The Glens need to play like a top-6 team from here on out -- and they need a little help from other results.

Focus on the Elk Grove and Napa games. Everything else is either expected (wins vs. bottom teams) or a bonus (Burlingame). Those two matchups against direct rivals will determine whether the season ends with a realistic shot at top 6 or a mid-table finish.
